Transport Guide

Japan bus guide for tourists.

Buses can help in rural areas, airports, theme parks and places where trains do not reach directly.

Quick answer

Japanese buses vary by area. Check whether you board from the front or back, keep your IC card or cash ready and confirm the stop name.

  • Check front-door or rear-door boarding.
  • Prepare IC card or coins.
  • Watch the stop display.
  • Press the stop button before getting off.
  • Use highway buses for budget long-distance routes.

Use buses when trains are not enough

Local busesUseful outside major stationsLocal buses help reach temples, museums, hotels and rural attractions.
Highway busesOften cheaper than trainsHighway buses can save money for long-distance travel, but take more time.
PaymentRules vary by areaSome buses charge flat fares, while others calculate by distance.
