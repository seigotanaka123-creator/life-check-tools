Transport Guide

Japan walking directions guide for tourists.

Walking in Japan is easier when you check exits, landmarks and underground paths before leaving the station.

Quick answer

The biggest walking mistake is using the wrong station exit. Check exit numbers, save screenshots and use landmarks when streets feel confusing.

  • Check the station exit number first.
  • Save offline screenshots.
  • Use convenience stores as landmarks.
  • Expect underground passages in big cities.
  • Give yourself extra time in large stations.
