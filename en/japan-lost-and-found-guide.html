Safety Guide

Japan lost and found guide for tourists.

Japan has strong lost-and-found systems, but you need the right station, shop or police box.

Quick answer

Know where to ask when you lose a phone, wallet, bag or train item.

  • Return to the last station, shop or restaurant first.
  • Ask station staff for train items.
  • Use a police box for lost valuables.
  • Prepare a simple item description.
  • Keep passport and card backup information separate.

Where to ask

TrainAsk station staffTrain items are often handled by station or railway lost-and-found offices.
StreetUse a police boxFor wallets, phones and street losses, a koban may help.
