Transport Guide

Japan shinkansen guide for tourists.

Understand tickets, seats and luggage before taking the shinkansen between major cities.

Quick answer

The shinkansen is easier when you know your station, train name, car number and seat. Arrive early enough to find the platform and buy food.

  • Check departure station carefully.
  • Reserve seats for busy travel days.
  • Know oversized luggage rules.
  • Arrive early for platform and food.
  • Keep your ticket until the exit gate.
