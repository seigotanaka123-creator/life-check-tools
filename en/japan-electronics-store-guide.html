Shopping Guide

Japan electronics store guide.

Electronics stores in Japan are useful for chargers, cameras, travel adapters, headphones and tax-free shopping, but tourists should check compatibility before buying.

Quick answer

Before buying electronics in Japan, check voltage, plug type, language settings, warranty coverage and whether tax-free packaging rules affect your trip.

  • Check voltage and plug compatibility.
  • Confirm language and region settings.
  • Ask whether warranty works outside Japan.
  • Prepare passport for tax-free shopping.
  • Do not open sealed tax-free items if rules prohibit it.

Compatibility checks

  • Look for input voltage on chargers and devices.
  • Check whether the product menu supports your language.
  • Confirm if the warranty is Japan-only or international.
  • Check whether the item is locked to Japanese services or standards.
  • Keep receipts and tax-free documents organized.

Common mistakes

  • Buying a device without checking language settings.
  • Assuming the warranty works in your home country.
  • Forgetting luggage space for boxed electronics.
  • Opening sealed tax-free items too early.
  • Buying hair tools without checking voltage and heat settings.
