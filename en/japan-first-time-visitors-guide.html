First Trip Guide

Japan guide for first-time visitors.

Start with the decisions that make your first Japan trip easier: where to stay, how to move, what to prepare and what to avoid.

Quick answer

For a first Japan trip, you do not need to know everything. Focus on transport, internet, payment, manners, food, hotel location and a realistic route.

  • Choose a practical hotel area
  • Prepare internet before arrival
  • Learn basic train and IC card use
  • Carry some cash
  • Keep the itinerary realistic

What to prepare first

RouteDo not move hotels too oftenChanging cities every day can make the trip feel busy. Choose a few main bases and build around them.
TransportUnderstand trains before day oneYou do not need to master every line, but station exits, IC cards and route apps help a lot.
MoneyUse both card and cashCards work in many places, but small shops, lockers and older restaurants may still need cash.
MannersLearn a few basic habitsQuiet trains, lines, trash rules and simple restaurant manners can reduce stress.
FoodPlan easy meals tooMix famous restaurants with convenience stores, casual chains and station food so every meal is not a project.

FAQ

What should I know before visiting Japan for the first time?Start with basic manners, train use, cash and card habits, internet access, hotel location, food choices and a realistic itinerary.
Is Japan easy for first-time visitors?Japan can be comfortable for first-time visitors if you prepare transport, internet, payment options and a few simple phrases before the trip.
How should I plan my first Japan trip?Choose a few main bases, avoid changing hotels too often, and prepare key decisions before arrival instead of trying to learn everything on the first day.
