Transport Guide

Japan airport transfer guide for tourists.

Compare airport trains, buses, taxis and hotel access so your first transfer in Japan feels manageable.

Quick answer

The best airport transfer depends on arrival time, luggage, hotel area and budget. Check the route before the flight, then keep one backup option.

  • Check your arrival airport and terminal.
  • Confirm the final station near your hotel.
  • Use buses when they stop close to your hotel.
  • Use taxis carefully for late arrivals or heavy luggage.
  • Save the hotel address offline.
