Food Guide

Japan food allergy guide for tourists.

Food allergies need extra preparation in Japan because ingredients and communication can be difficult while traveling.

Quick answer

Bring allergy cards in Japanese, check packaged food labels carefully and avoid risky restaurants when staff cannot confirm ingredients clearly.

  • Prepare allergy cards in Japanese.
  • Use translation apps as backup.
  • Check labels on packaged food.
  • Avoid unclear sauces and broths.
  • Carry emergency medicine if prescribed.
