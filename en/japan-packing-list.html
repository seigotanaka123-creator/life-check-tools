Packing List

Japan packing list for a comfortable first trip.

Pack lighter, walk easier and leave room for souvenirs. This checklist focuses on what helps most during a real Japan trip.

Quick answer

Use this Japan packing list for clothes, shoes, cash, travel documents, adapters, medicine, luggage, convenience store items and seasonal travel basics.

  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Passport, card and some cash
  • Portable charger
  • Small towel and tissues
  • Foldable bag for souvenirs

What to know

TipComfortable shoesYou will walk more than expected in stations, shopping streets, temples and neighborhoods. Comfortable shoes matter more than stylish shoes.
TipCash, cards and documentsBring a credit card, some cash, passport, travel insurance details and digital copies of important information.
TipSmall daily bag itemsCarry a small towel, tissues, hand sanitizer, water, a portable charger and a small trash bag for wrappers.
TipClothes by seasonJapan changes a lot by season. Summer can be hot and humid, winter can be dry and cold, and spring or autumn may need layers.
TipLeave suitcase spaceSouvenirs, snacks, stationery and small gifts add up quickly. Leave empty space or bring a foldable bag.

FAQ

What should I pack for Japan?Pack comfortable shoes, passport, card, cash, portable charger, small towel, tissues, medicine, season-appropriate clothes and extra space for souvenirs.
Do I need to bring a lot of toiletries to Japan?You can buy many basic toiletries at convenience stores, drugstores and supermarkets, so you do not need to overpack them.
Should I bring a large suitcase to Japan?A large suitcase is possible, but it can be stressful on trains and stairs. Packing lighter or using luggage forwarding is often easier.
