Japan ramen guide for tourists.

Ramen is easier when you know ticket machines, counter seats, toppings and line etiquette.

Quick answer

Many ramen shops are small and quick. Choose a dish, use the ticket machine if needed, sit where guided and avoid staying long after eating.

  • Check if there is a ticket machine.
  • Choose basic ramen if unsure.
  • Wait in line neatly.
  • Use counter seats calmly.
  • Leave after eating during busy times.
