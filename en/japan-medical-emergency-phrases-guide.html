Emergency Guide

Japan medical emergency phrases guide.

If you feel unwell in Japan, clear written information matters. Prepare simple phrases, allergy details, medicine names and hotel contact information before you need them.

Quick answer

Save key medical phrases in Japanese and English, carry allergy and medicine information, and ask hotel staff or emergency services for help if symptoms are serious.

  • Save hotel address and phone number.
  • Carry medicine and allergy details.
  • Use short phrases and translation screenshots.
  • Ask staff to help contact medical services.
  • Call emergency help for serious symptoms.

Useful phrase types

IllnessI feel sickPrepare symptoms such as fever, pain, nausea or dizziness.
AllergyI have an allergyCarry written allergen names and severity.
HelpPlease call for helpHotel staff, station staff or restaurant staff may help in emergencies.

Prepare before travel

  • List regular medicines and generic names if possible.
  • Save travel insurance and emergency contacts offline.
  • Prepare allergy cards for food and medicine allergies.
  • Keep passport, insurance and hotel details easy to access.
  • Know that emergency numbers and hospital flow may differ from home.

Common mistakes

  • Keeping all medical information only in an app that needs internet.
  • Not knowing medicine names or dosage.
  • Waiting too long to ask for help because of language worries.
  • Relying on vague symptoms instead of written details.
  • Forgetting travel insurance contact information.
