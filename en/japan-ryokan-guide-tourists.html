Japan ryokan guide for tourists.

Ryokan stays are memorable when you understand meals, rooms, futons, slippers and onsen manners.

Quick answer

A ryokan is different from a hotel. Check meal times, bath rules, footwear and futon setup before assuming everything works like a standard hotel.

  • Confirm dinner and breakfast times.
  • Remove shoes where required.
  • Use slippers only in allowed areas.
  • Check onsen rules before bathing.
  • Ask staff if unsure about futons or meals.
