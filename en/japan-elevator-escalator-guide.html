Station Guide

Japan elevator and escalator guide.

Elevators and escalators can decide whether a station route is easy or exhausting, especially with luggage, strollers, children or mobility concerns.

Quick answer

Check elevator routes before leaving a station with luggage or strollers. On escalators, stand safely, hold handrails and follow local flow without blocking others.

  • Look for elevator signs before committing to stairs.
  • Use elevators patiently with luggage or strollers.
  • Keep suitcases stable on escalators.
  • Do not stop suddenly at escalator exits.
  • Leave priority space for people who need elevators most.

Common mistakes

  • Dragging large suitcases up stairs because the elevator was not checked.
  • Stopping at the top or bottom of an escalator to check maps.
  • Blocking elevator doors with luggage.
  • Taking elevators for convenience while people with priority needs are waiting.
  • Assuming every station exit has elevator access.

FAQ

Do all station exits have elevators?No. Check accessible exits before leaving the station.
Can I use escalators with luggage?You can, but elevators are safer with large or heavy suitcases.
Should I stand on one side?Local habits vary, and safety guidance may ask people not to walk. Follow signs and avoid blocking exits.
