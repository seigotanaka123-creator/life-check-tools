Safety Guide

Japan earthquake preparedness guide for tourists.

A simple earthquake plan helps tourists stay calmer during alerts, shaking or transport disruption.

Quick answer

Know what to do during an earthquake and how to prepare basic backups.

  • Follow local staff instructions.
  • Protect your head and stay away from glass.
  • Do not rush outside during shaking.
  • Save hotel and embassy information offline.
  • Keep phone battery and water when possible.
