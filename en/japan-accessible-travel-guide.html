Access Guide

Japan accessible travel guide for tourists.

Accessible travel in Japan is possible, but it requires careful route checks. Elevators, station exits, hotel rooms and attraction access can vary widely.

Quick answer

Plan accessible routes by confirming elevator exits, hotel room details, station transfers and attraction access in advance. Do not rely only on shortest route estimates.

  • Check elevator routes, not only station names.
  • Confirm barrier-free hotel room details directly.
  • Allow extra time for transfers and staff assistance.
  • Check attraction access before booking timed tickets.
  • Keep backup transport options such as taxis.

What to verify

StationsElevator exitsThe accessible exit may be farther than the closest exit.
HotelsRoom detailsConfirm bathroom, bed height, door width and elevator access.
AttractionsTerrain variesTemples, castles, gardens and old streets may have stairs, gravel or slopes.

Common mistakes

  • Trusting a route app without checking elevator paths.
  • Booking a hotel room labeled accessible without confirming details.
  • Planning old temples or castles without checking terrain.
  • Not leaving time for station staff help.
  • Carrying too much luggage while managing mobility needs.
