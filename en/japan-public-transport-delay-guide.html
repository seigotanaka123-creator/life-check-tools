Transport Guide

Japan public transport delay guide.

Japan's transport is reliable, but delays still happen. A calm backup plan protects flights, reservations and last trains.

Quick answer

When transport is delayed in Japan, check official updates, confirm the route with staff or apps, add buffer time and avoid risky last-minute transfers.

  • Check both apps and station signs.
  • Ask staff before changing routes.
  • Leave extra time before flights.
  • Know your last train or bus.
  • Save hotel and reservation contacts.

What to do first

  • Stay near a staffed gate or information board if you are unsure.
  • Check route apps, railway websites and station announcements.
  • Confirm direction, platform and ticket validity before boarding another route.
  • Contact your hotel, restaurant or tour if delay affects arrival time.
  • Consider waiting if the original line is expected to restart soon.

Common mistakes and cautions

  • Following crowd movement without knowing the destination.
  • Forgetting that last trains leave earlier than many visitors expect.
  • Booking airport transfers with no delay buffer.
  • Assuming a delay certificate solves missed reservations or flights.
  • Ignoring ferry or bus suspensions during severe weather.

Delay checklist

  • Official route status checked.
  • Backup route and cost confirmed.
  • Important reservations contacted.
  • Battery and mobile data preserved.
  • Last train, taxi or hotel option reviewed.

FAQ

Can staff help in English?Major stations often have some English support. Screenshots and destination names help.
Should I take a taxi during delays?Sometimes, but taxis can be scarce and expensive during major disruption.
How much buffer should I add?Add generous time before airports, tours and dinner reservations, especially when transfers are involved.
