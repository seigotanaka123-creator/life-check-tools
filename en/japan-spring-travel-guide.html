Japan spring travel guide for tourists.

Spring is beautiful but popular, so flexible routes and weather backups make travel easier.

Quick answer

Cherry blossom timing changes each year. Book early, expect crowds and keep backup plans in case weather or bloom timing shifts.

  • Check cherry blossom forecasts.
  • Book hotels early for popular dates.
  • Expect crowded parks and stations.
  • Bring layers for changing weather.
  • Keep rainy-day backups.
