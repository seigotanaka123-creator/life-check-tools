Weather Guide

Japan weather clothing guide for tourists.

Japan travel often includes long walks, train transfers and weather changes, so practical clothing matters.

Quick answer

Choose clothes by season, walking time and rain risk. Comfortable shoes and layers often matter more than a perfect outfit.

  • Check weather by city.
  • Bring comfortable walking shoes.
  • Use layers in spring and autumn.
  • Prepare humidity protection in summer.
  • Plan warm socks and layers in winter.
