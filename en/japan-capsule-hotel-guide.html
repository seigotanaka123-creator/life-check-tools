Stay Guide

Japan capsule hotel guide for tourists.

Capsule hotels can be useful for short stays, solo travel and late arrivals when you understand the rules.

Quick answer

Capsule hotels are compact and rule-based. They are best for short stays or solo travelers, not for large luggage or private room space.

  • Check gender floor rules.
  • Store luggage in the assigned area.
  • Keep noise low.
  • Use shared showers respectfully.
  • Confirm check-in and checkout times.
