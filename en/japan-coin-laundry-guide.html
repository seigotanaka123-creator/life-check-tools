Japan coin laundry guide for tourists.

Coin laundries help tourists pack less and refresh clothes during longer Japan trips.

Quick answer

Coin laundries are useful, but machines, detergent and payment vary. Check whether the machine washes and dries together, and avoid leaving clothes too long.

  • Check if detergent is automatic.
  • Bring coins or check cashless payment.
  • Confirm wash and dry time.
  • Do not leave clothes after finishing.
  • Use hotel laundry rooms when available.
