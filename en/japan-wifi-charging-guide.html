Japan Wi-Fi and charging guide for tourists.

Internet and battery backup matter because maps, trains, translation and payments often depend on your phone.

Quick answer

Do not rely only on public Wi-Fi. Prepare eSIM, SIM or pocket Wi-Fi, carry a power bank and save important screenshots.

  • Choose eSIM, SIM or pocket Wi-Fi before arrival.
  • Carry a charged power bank.
  • Do not rely only on public Wi-Fi.
  • Save route screenshots offline.
  • Check outlet type and charging cables.
