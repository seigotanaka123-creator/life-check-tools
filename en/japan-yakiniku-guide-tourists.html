Food Guide

Japan yakiniku guide for tourists.

Yakiniku is Japanese-style grilled meat where diners cook pieces at the table. It is fun, but ordering, cooking and all-you-can-eat rules can confuse first-time visitors.

Quick answer

Order meat and sides, grill small portions at the table, use tongs for raw meat, avoid overordering and check time limits if the restaurant is all-you-can-eat.

  • Use tongs for raw meat and chopsticks for eating.
  • Cook small batches instead of filling the grill.
  • Check sauces, rice and side dishes.
  • Watch all-you-can-eat time limits.
  • Check smoke, clothing and smell before plans after dinner.

How yakiniku works

OrderMeat by cutMenus may list beef, pork, chicken, organs, vegetables and set plates.
GrillCook at the tableTurn meat carefully and avoid burning thin pieces.
RulesTime limits may applyAll-you-can-eat plans often have last-order and time rules.

Common mistakes

  • Using eating chopsticks for raw meat.
  • Ordering too much before understanding portion sizes.
  • Letting meat burn while taking photos.
  • Ignoring all-you-can-eat time limits.
  • Choosing yakiniku before an activity where smoke smell matters.
