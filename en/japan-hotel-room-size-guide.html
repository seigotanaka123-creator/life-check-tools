Japan hotel room size guide.

Japanese hotel rooms can be smaller than many visitors expect. Checking room size, bed layout and luggage space before booking prevents daily frustration.

Quick answer

Before booking, check square meters, bed type, luggage space, bathroom layout and child policy. A cheap room far from the station or too small for bags may not be a good value.

  • Check room size in square meters.
  • Confirm bed count and bed width.
  • Think about where suitcases will open.
  • Check bathroom style and amenities.
  • For families, confirm child and extra bed rules.

Common mistakes

  • Booking by price only without checking room size.
  • Assuming two large suitcases can open easily.
  • Confusing double bed with room for two people and luggage.
  • Ignoring station access to save a small amount.
  • Not checking child policy before arrival.
