Food Guide

Japan sushi guide for tourists.

Sushi can be casual or formal, so choose a style that matches your comfort, budget and language needs.

Quick answer

Conveyor belt sushi is usually easiest for first-time tourists. Counter sushi can be special, but it may require more budget and etiquette awareness.

  • Start with conveyor belt sushi if unsure.
  • Use tablets when available.
  • Do not overuse soy sauce.
  • Check plate prices or menu prices.
  • Ask before taking photos in formal shops.
