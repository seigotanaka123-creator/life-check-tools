Japan curry guide for tourists.

Japanese curry is a comforting, casual meal that is easy for many tourists, but toppings, spice levels and ticket-machine ordering can still be confusing.

Quick answer

Choose rice portion, curry type, topping and spice level if offered. If unsure, start with standard curry and one topping such as katsu, egg or vegetables.

  • Check rice size before ordering large portions.
  • Choose spice level carefully.
  • Use ticket machines if the shop has one.
  • Check allergy concerns because curry roux may contain wheat, dairy or meat.
  • Use curry shops for quick solo or casual meals.

Common mistakes

  • Ordering large rice without realizing portion size.
  • Choosing a high spice level because it sounds fun.
  • Assuming vegetable curry is vegetarian.
  • Missing the ticket machine at the entrance.
  • Forgetting that curry can stain clothes during travel days.
