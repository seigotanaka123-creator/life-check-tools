Planning Guide

Japan ticket reservation guide for tourists.

Popular trains, museums, theme parks and restaurants may need reservations before the day.

Quick answer

Reserve important tickets early and keep backup plans for busy days.

  • Check whether advance tickets are required.
  • Save QR codes and confirmation emails.
  • Confirm cancellation and time-slot rules.
  • Prepare backup spots nearby.
  • Avoid stacking too many timed reservations.
