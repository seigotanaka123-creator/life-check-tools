Onsen Guide

Japan tattoo onsen guide for tourists.

Tattoo rules at Japanese onsen vary by facility. The safest approach is to check policy before booking and choose private or tattoo-friendly baths when needed.

Quick answer

If you have tattoos, do not assume public baths will accept them. Check the official policy, ask your ryokan, use cover stickers when allowed or book private baths.

  • Check tattoo rules before booking.
  • Use private baths when public rules are unclear.
  • Ask whether cover stickers are accepted.
  • Do not hide tattoos against stated rules.
  • Follow normal onsen washing manners.

Common mistakes

  • Booking a ryokan without checking tattoo policy.
  • Assuming private room means private bath.
  • Using cover stickers where they are not accepted.
  • Arguing with staff at the bath entrance.
  • Forgetting that pool, spa and gym rules may differ.
