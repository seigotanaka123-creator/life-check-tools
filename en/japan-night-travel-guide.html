Safety Guide

Japan night travel guide for tourists.

Night travel is easier when you know the last train, taxi backup and route back to your hotel.

Quick answer

Before staying out late, check your last train and taxi backup. Save the hotel address and avoid long unfamiliar walks when tired.

  • Check last train times early.
  • Save your hotel address.
  • Keep taxi money backup.
  • Use well-lit main streets.
  • Buy late food from convenience stores if needed.
