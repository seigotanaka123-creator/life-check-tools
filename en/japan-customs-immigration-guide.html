Quick answer

Understand the airport arrival flow and what to prepare before passport control.

  • Prepare passport and arrival information.
  • Check customs rules before packing.
  • Save hotel address offline.
  • Follow airport signs for immigration and customs.
  • Keep one transfer backup after arrival.
