Transport Guide

Japan domestic flight guide for tourists.

Domestic flights can save time on long routes, but airport access and luggage rules matter.

Quick answer

Use domestic flights wisely for long-distance travel inside Japan.

  • Compare total door-to-door time.
  • Check baggage rules before booking.
  • Leave enough airport transfer time.
  • Save booking details offline.
  • Keep a backup plan for delays.

When flights help

DistanceLong routes may be fasterFlights can help for Hokkaido, Kyushu, Okinawa and long cross-country moves.
AirportAccess mattersA cheap flight is less useful if airport transfers are long or expensive.
