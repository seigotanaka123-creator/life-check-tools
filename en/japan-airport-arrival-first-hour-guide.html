Arrival Guide

Japan airport arrival first hour guide.

The first hour after landing in Japan can feel busy: immigration, luggage, cash, internet, IC cards and transport all compete for attention. A simple order makes arrival much calmer.

Quick answer

After landing, clear immigration and customs first, collect luggage, set up internet, get some yen if needed, confirm your transport route and avoid making big decisions while tired.

  • Finish immigration, customs and baggage before extra errands.
  • Set up internet before leaving the airport.
  • Withdraw or exchange enough yen for the first day.
  • Confirm train, bus or taxi route to your hotel.
  • Do not overpack the first evening with reservations.

The calm arrival order

Many arrival mistakes happen because travelers try to solve everything at once. Treat the first hour as a short checklist, not a sightseeing race.

Step 1Immigration and customsKeep passport, arrival information and luggage documents easy to reach.
Step 2Luggage and essentialsCheck your bags before leaving the secure area and keep valuables with you.
Step 3Internet and moneySet up eSIM, SIM or pocket Wi-Fi, then get enough yen for immediate needs.

Before leaving the airport

  • Confirm your hotel address and nearest station.
  • Save screenshots of your route in case mobile data fails.
  • Check whether an airport train, limousine bus or taxi is easiest with luggage.
  • Buy or charge an IC card if it fits your route.
  • Use the restroom and buy water before a long transfer.

Choose the first transfer by luggage and fatigue

The best first route is not always the cheapest or fastest. Choose the option that gets you to the hotel calmly with your bags.

What to postpone

Do not try to solve every trip detail at the airport. Tax-free shopping, complicated restaurant bookings and long sightseeing detours can wait. Your first priority is reaching your hotel or first base safely with working internet and payment backup.

Common mistakes

  • Leaving the airport without working internet or saved route screenshots.
  • Trying to take the cheapest route even with heavy luggage and jet lag.
  • Not getting any yen before heading to a small hotel or late-night area.
  • Booking a strict timed activity shortly after international arrival.
  • Forgetting that airport terminals and train platforms can take time to navigate.
