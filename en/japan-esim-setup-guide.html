Internet Guide

Japan eSIM setup guide for tourists.

An eSIM can make arrival in Japan much easier, but setup timing, QR codes and phone compatibility should be handled before you are tired at the airport.

Quick answer

Buy and prepare your Japan eSIM before departure, save the QR code and instructions offline, confirm your phone supports eSIM and know whether activation starts at purchase or first connection.

  • Check phone compatibility before buying.
  • Save QR code and setup instructions offline.
  • Install using reliable Wi-Fi before or at arrival.
  • Confirm activation timing and data period.
  • Keep airport Wi-Fi or screenshots as backup.

Arrival setup tips

  • Use airport Wi-Fi if mobile data is not active yet.
  • Keep your primary SIM settings clear to avoid roaming surprises.
  • Test maps and messaging before leaving the airport.
  • Save hotel address and transfer route even after eSIM works.
  • Keep provider support instructions available offline.

Common mistakes

  • Buying an eSIM without checking phone compatibility.
  • Keeping the QR code only inside an email that needs internet.
  • Activating too early and wasting plan days.
  • Leaving the airport before confirming maps work.
  • Forgetting battery backup while relying on the phone for everything.
