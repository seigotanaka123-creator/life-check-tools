Season Guide

Japan summer travel guide for tourists.

Summer in Japan can be fun, but heat, humidity and sudden weather changes need realistic planning.

Quick answer

Plan shorter outdoor blocks, drink often and keep indoor backup stops. Summer travel is easier when you avoid the hottest hours.

  • Carry water and use vending machines.
  • Plan indoor breaks.
  • Check rain and typhoon forecasts.
  • Wear breathable clothing.
  • Avoid overloading midday outdoor plans.
