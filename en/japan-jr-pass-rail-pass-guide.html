Transport Guide

Japan rail pass guide for tourists.

Compare rail passes with your route before buying, because not every Japan trip needs a pass.

Quick answer

Decide whether a rail pass fits your route, timing and budget.

  • List long-distance train days first.
  • Compare normal fares with pass price.
  • Check which trains are covered.
  • Consider regional passes for focused trips.
  • Do not buy a pass just because it sounds convenient.

Before you buy

RoutePasses depend on distanceA pass helps most when you make several long train trips.
BudgetCompare carefullyShort city trips may be cheaper without a national pass.
