Travel Services

Useful services in Japan that make travel easier.

Know the everyday services that can reduce stress during arrival, train travel, hotel changes, shopping and busy sightseeing days.

Quick answer

Japan has many services that help tourists, but they are easiest to use when you know they exist before the trip.

  • Use convenience stores for daily needs
  • Prepare IC cards or payment options
  • Use coin lockers on transfer days
  • Consider luggage forwarding
  • Keep apps and screenshots ready

Helpful services to know

DailyConvenience storesUse them for simple meals, drinks, ATMs, umbrellas, chargers and quick supplies.
TransportIC cards and route appsCards and route apps make trains, buses and small purchases easier to manage.
LuggageCoin lockersLockers help when you arrive before check-in or want to explore without bags.
LuggageLuggage forwardingSending suitcases between hotels can make long train days much calmer.
BackupInformation counters and screenshotsStation counters, airport desks and saved screenshots can help when apps or internet fail.

FAQ

What services are useful for tourists in Japan?Useful services include convenience stores, IC cards, coin lockers, luggage forwarding, station information counters, airport services and translation apps.
Is luggage forwarding useful in Japan?Yes. Luggage forwarding can make train travel and hotel changes easier, especially when moving between cities.
Can convenience stores help tourists in Japan?Yes. Convenience stores are useful for meals, drinks, ATMs, basic goods and quick travel-day needs.
