Japan hotel check-in guide for tourists.

Know check-in times, luggage storage, passports, room size and late arrival basics before booking.

Quick answer

Avoid hotel surprises around check-in, luggage and room expectations.

  • Check check-in and checkout times.
  • Confirm luggage storage options.
  • Keep passport ready.
  • Tell the hotel if arriving late.
  • Check room size before booking.
