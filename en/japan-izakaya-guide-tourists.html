Japan izakaya guide for tourists.

Izakaya are casual places for drinks and shared dishes, but tourists should know seating charges and ordering style.

Quick answer

Izakaya are not just restaurants. Expect drinks, shared plates and sometimes a small seating charge or appetizer charge called otoshi.

  • Check if there is a seating charge.
  • Order drinks and shared dishes.
  • Use tablets or call staff when needed.
  • Check smoking rules.
  • Split payment may not always be easy.
