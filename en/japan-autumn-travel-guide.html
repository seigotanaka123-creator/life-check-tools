Season Guide

Japan autumn travel guide for tourists.

Autumn is a popular season for walking, temples, parks and seasonal food when routes are planned realistically.

Quick answer

Autumn is often comfortable, but timing varies by region. Check foliage forecasts, bring layers and expect crowds at famous leaf spots.

  • Check autumn leaf timing by area.
  • Bring layers for morning and evening.
  • Expect crowds at famous spots.
  • Book popular routes early.
  • Use parks and temples for seasonal walks.
